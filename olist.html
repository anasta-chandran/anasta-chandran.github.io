01 · Project Overview

From e-commerce data to business understanding.

This project explored the Olist e-commerce dataset to understand how sales, customer activity, payment patterns and delivery performance relate to the overall shopping experience.

The analysis brought together multiple datasets, including customers, orders, order items, payments, reviews, products and sellers, to examine different aspects of the business.

The objective was to turn raw transactional data into meaningful findings that could support better business decisions.

02 · Business Problem

What can e-commerce data reveal?

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of information through orders, payments, product sales, customer feedback and deliveries. Looking at these records separately can make it difficult to understand overall business performance.

This project examined how these connected datasets could reveal sales patterns, customer satisfaction indicators, payment preferences and operational performance.

07 · Key Findings

What the analysis revealed.

Delivery and satisfaction

Orders with a 1-star review had an average shipping time of approximately 21.25 days, compared with 10.62 days for 5-star reviews.

Category performance

Health & Beauty was among the leading product categories, with approximately ₹1.26 million in sales.

Payment patterns

Reported weekday payment value was approximately ₹12.37 million, compared with ₹3.64 million on weekends.

Category delivery

Pet Shop orders recorded an average delivery time of approximately 11.31 days.

Business interpretation

The relationship between delivery duration and review scores suggests that delivery experience deserves attention when evaluating customer satisfaction. These patterns are associations observed in the analysis, not proof of causation.
